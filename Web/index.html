      How it works

      Pencil plans, fingers navigate. Everything saves automatically.

      Draw a blockIn Blocks mode, drag your Pencil down a day. A quick tap adds a one-hour block. Times snap to 15 minutes.
      Write the titleJust write in the title field with your Pencil. iPad turns handwriting into text (Scribble).
      Move and resizeDrag a block with the Pencil to move it, even to another day. Drag its top or bottom edge to change the time.
      FingersSwipe to scroll. Tap a block to edit it. Press and hold a block to pick it up, or an empty slot to add one.
      Ink and EraseSwitch to Ink to handwrite notes anywhere on the week. In Erase, rub out ink or tap a block to delete it. Double-tap your Pencil to switch between Erase and Blocks.
      SelectCircle blocks with the Pencil to select them, then drag one to move them all, or mark them done, recolour or delete them.
      Repeat on other daysOpen a block and tap days under “Repeat on” to copy it, like a gym session on Mon, Wed and Fri.
      PresetsSave a day or a whole week you like and apply it again in one tap, for example your office day on Mon to Thu. Tap a day’s name for quick options.
      To-do listWrite tasks in the to-do panel. Drag one onto the week with your Pencil to schedule it. Ticking off either the to-do or its block marks both done.
